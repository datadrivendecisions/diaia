# LRD logbook

What changed in the integrated LRD (`site/integrated-lrd.html`), and why. The
document itself carries only a version number and a date, so that it reads as a
current statement rather than a history of itself. Newest entry first. Bump the
version in the LRD and add a row here in the same commit.

| Version | Date | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 1.2 | 4 September 2026 | The modules are renamed AIBS (AI in Business & Society) and AEL (Agent Engineering Lab), per ADR-0008; the previous names DIAIA and AEAIS are removed from the document, and Appendix A records the current module by its course code only. The version history that used to sit in the document's Version row moves to this logbook. The document is published on GitHub Pages at https://datadrivendecisions.github.io/diaia/integrated-lrd.html so it can be shared by link; its references to the sketches, the proposal and the decision records become GitHub URLs. |
| 1.1 | 4 September 2026 | Incorporates the research module owner's vision from the *Voorstel Handbook DIAIA*: the research module becomes a research module with a fixed thematic spine, six teaching weeks plus a consolidation week, assessed on six applied-research capabilities. Follows decision records ADR-0001 to ADR-0007 and cites them where they bind. Both module owners are named as authors, each responsible for their own module's outcomes, criteria and mark. Appendix A absorbs the former `module-overview.md`, the record of the module as it runs today. |
| 1.0 | 7 July 2026 | First version. Reads the research module as a taught strategy/economics/society course with a fixed weekly programme, and commits the sister module to Sketch 3 of the redesign sketches. The next day the business and technical roles were merged into one team of four, and durable-skills grounding was added. |
